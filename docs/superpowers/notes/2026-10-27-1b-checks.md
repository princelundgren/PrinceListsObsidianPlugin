# Step 1b checks

Plan step 1b, Task 5, Steps 1 and 2. Every check that was run passed.

- **On the Mac:** scripted through Obsidian's CLI (`obsidian eval`) in a test vault, with real key
  presses and clicks sent through its `dev:cdp` command.
- **On the iPhone:** read through Safari's Web Inspector, bridged with `ios_webkit_debug_proxy`.
  The drag and the photo were done by hand, and a recorder in the page logged what happened.
- **Versions:** Obsidian 1.14.4 on the Mac and 1.14.5 on the iPhone, with iOS 18.7. The plugin was
  built from `main` after Task 4 merged.

## Step 1: the iPhone

The checks of Task 2's and Task 3's Step 6 ran on each pull request's own build and are recorded
there: [#29](https://github.com/princelundgren/PrinceListsObsidianPlugin/pull/29) for dragging and
[#30](https://github.com/princelundgren/PrinceListsObsidianPlugin/pull/30) for pictures. On the
build from `main`, with Tasks and Kanban on:

- **A drag with the edge scroll:** the bottom item, held and dragged to the top edge, scrolled the
  list from 1,020 px to the top and dropped above the first item. Only that item moved in the
  file. Scrolling selected no text and opened no menu.
- **A photo dragged from Photos onto an open card's notes:** saved as a JPEG in the vault's
  attachment folder and linked in the item's notes, with no notice. iOS showed the drag's types
  only as `Files` until the drop, as the design expects.
- **Risk 6, the `✔` glyph:** in reading view on the iPhone it shows as Apple's gray check mark. It
  reads as a check mark and doesn't stand out. On the Mac it reads as a text check mark (1a
  checks).

## Step 2: alongside Tasks and Kanban

- **Setup:** Tasks and Kanban turned on for the session, on both devices.
- **Opening:** the list still opens as a list, on the Mac and the iPhone, read through each one's
  script.
- **Undo on the Mac:** a tick, then Cmd+Z, and a drag, then Cmd+Z, each leave the file byte for
  byte as it was.
- **A mouse drag on the Mac:** dragged to the bottom edge of the list, it scrolls the list. The
  maintainer checked this by hand.
- **The drag on the iPhone:** above.
- **Tasks query,** on the Mac by script, in reading view: `not done` with `path includes <list>`
  lists all 42 open items, with their ⏳ and 📅 dates.

## Not checked

- An iPhone with a hardware keyboard, where Cmd+Z would reach the list.
- A popout window, for pictures and the keys.
- A user hotkey bound to Cmd+Z, under the view's scope.
