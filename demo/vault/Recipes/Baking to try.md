- [ ] cinnamon buns ⭐

	1. Make the dough and let it rise for an hour.
	2. Roll it out, spread the butter, sugar and cinnamon.
	3. Roll up, slice, rise again for 30 minutes.
	4. Bake at `200 °C` for 12 minutes.
